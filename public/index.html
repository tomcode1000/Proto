A licence can die in month seven.

Most contractors verify a subcontractor once, at onboarding, then file the certificate. Nobody is told when the state changes that status — so the work continues, the draws continue, and the exposure sits with the general contractor.

01

Contracts stop working

An agreement with an unlicensed contractor can be void and unenforceable — and the lien goes with it.

02

Cover can lapse

Most general liability policies exclude work performed in violation of licensing law.

03

The citation is yours

Under OSHA's multi-employer policy the GC is the controlling employer on the site.

Facts first. Judgement second.

Connectors and a unit-tested scoring engine establish what is true. The agent layer is handed findings that are already verified, and only decides what to do first and what to write. Nothing downstream can change a status, a date, or a permit count.

REGISTRY

Read the record

Live Florida DBPR status and county permit activity, queried per subcontractor — never cached into a stale copy.

ENGINE

Rank real danger

A dead licence carrying open work outranks a dormant one expiring next week. Every reason is recomputed from the record.

AGENTS

Say what to do

A Strands graph orders the morning and drafts the notice. It cannot invent a number that isn't in the findings.

Licence status and permit activity are public records published by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation and county permit services.
