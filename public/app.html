Who is on this project

Paste the licence numbers you want watched. Proto looks each one up as it imports, so the business name comes from the state register rather than from whoever typed the list.

Keeping the list in a Google Sheet instead? Open Roster to connect one, or to add a single licence and remove people.

How often should Proto check?

A licence verified once is a snapshot. Verified on a schedule it becomes a watch, which is the whole point, Proto re-checks on its own and speaks up only when something changed.

Where should Proto reach you

Optional. The first check sends the whole roster, with every licence number, class, status and expiry date. After that each check reports what moved, and says so plainly when nothing did, so you can always tell working from broken.

Licences come from the Florida DBPR register; permit activity from county ArcGIS services. Both are public records, so there is nothing to connect and no key to manage.
